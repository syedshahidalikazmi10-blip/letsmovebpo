Data Protection

Your customers' details are safe with us.

Handling personal data is a responsibility. We take UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 seriously, and we agree the safeguards in writing before any work begins.

1

Written agreement first

Where we handle personal data for you, a data processing agreement is signed before work starts.

2

You stay in control

You remain the data controller. We act only on your instructions, for the agreed work.

3

Access you authorise

We use only the systems you approve. Logins stay confidential and you can withdraw access at any time.

4

Open about location

Our team works from Pakistan. We say so plainly and, where personal data is involved, agree appropriate transfer safeguards in writing before work starts, such as the UK IDTA or UK Addendum.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. We run a 7-day trial on an agreed shift pattern so you can judge the quality of our work before committing to anything.

Can you cover nights and weekends?

Yes. We cover late evenings, overnight, weekends, bank holidays and sickness gaps, and can arrange 24/7 rotas once hours and procedures are agreed in writing.

Which systems do you work on?

We work on leading taxi dispatch platforms such as iCabbi, Autocab, Cordic, CabTreasure, Cab9, CabX and Envoy, plus the booking, phone and diary systems you already use, within the access you authorise.

How is my customers' data protected?

We follow UK GDPR principles, agree a data processing agreement before handling personal data, use only approved accounts and keep logins confidential. The agreement sets out exactly what we do and what you remain responsible for.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is tailored to the services and hours you need and is confirmed in writing before we begin. Message us for a quote.
